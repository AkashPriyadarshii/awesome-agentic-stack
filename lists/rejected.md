# Rejected Repositories (The 290)

These are the 290 repositories that didn't survive the audit for the awesome-agentic-stack. Rejection reasons are explicit. If you think a rejection was wrong or a tool has updated its pricing/maintenance model, open a GitHub Issue.

---

## Rejection Reason Taxonomy
- **DEAD**: Last commit was more than 6 months ago.
- **CC_REQUIRED**: A free tier is claimed, but a credit card or payment method is required to register.
- **OVERLAP**: Performs the exact same role as another included tool, but is less performant, bulkier, or harder to set up.
- **SCOPE_CREEP**: Interesting concept, but not workflow-critical for active terminal or agent-based coding.
- **UNVERIFIABLE**: Repository stats, star counts, or functional claims could not be independently verified.
- **UNMAINTAINED**: Active issue backlog with zero maintainer responses or updates for over 3 months.
- **PAID_ONLY**: No meaningful free tier exists; requires paid subscription.
